Senator / State Senate / Nation Committees Methodology

A senator’s score comes from their recorded Senate votes. The state estimate comes from surveys of the state’s public. They are different measurement systems, so no distance between them is calculated until a validated bridge between the two scales exists.

About the three reference figures
    Medians (secondary comparison)

    Senate standing committees review bills in their subject area. This view describes where each committee’s current members sit compared with the Senate as a whole.

    Descriptive only: where a committee’s members sit says nothing on its own about what the committee did or decided.
